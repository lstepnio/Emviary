# Colorado bird coverage

The November 2026 catalog audit grouped artwork by scientific name and counted
only approved single-bird cutouts as illustrations that can appear alongside
other species. Historical plates remain approved where they already were, but
they display on their own. Approval in the catalog does not override a frame
owner's exclusions.

The source catalog had at least two entries per species, including plates
and artwork removed from the live library. The live library had 169 bird
illustrations for 63 species and ten species with a single illustration.
This first expansion adds two cutouts each for Mountain Chickadee,
White-crowned Sparrow, Wild Turkey, Bald Eagle, Great Blue Heron, Snowy
Egret and American Avocet. Each now has three illustrations in the live
library; previously removed artwork stays removed.

Brown Creeper, Black-crowned Night-Heron and Yellow-billed Cuckoo still
have one live illustration each. More cutouts for the first two would
help woodland and wetland coverage, respectively. The cuckoo is a lower
priority than familiar missing birds. [Colorado Parks and Wildlife lists
the western population as threatened](https://cpw.state.co.us/threatened-and-endangered-wildlife).

A second expansion adds Northern Flicker (western red-shafted form),
Woodhouse's Scrub-Jay, Chipping Sparrow and Cooper's Hawk, with three
illustrations per species. Chipping Sparrow's breeding-plumage art is
eligible April through September, a conservative window within its Colorado
spring to fall presence. The other three are eligible year-round.
Cooper's Hawk uses *Astur cooperii* in the catalog; acoustic reports under
*Accipiter cooperii* match the same species.

Priorities for future artwork, based on a comparison with regional field
checklists and the approved catalog:

| Priority | Missing from the bird catalog | Why add them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Summer visitors | Common Grackle, Cliff Swallow, Brown-headed Cowbird, Bullock's Oriole | Broaden seasonal coverage alongside the existing Red-winged Blackbird and Barn Swallow. |
| Warblers | Yellow-rumped Warbler, Wilson's Warbler | There are no approved wood-warbler species in the catalog. Start with conspicuous regional migrants. |
| Ponds and streams | Killdeer, Common Merganser, Green-winged Teal | Add an inland shorebird and more waterfowl beyond Mallard and Canada Goose. |
| Urban and alpine | Rock Pigeon, European Starling, White-tailed Ptarmigan | Fill two familiar introduced urban species and an iconic native alpine species with distinct winter and summer plumage. |

Among species with two live cutouts, Steller's Jay, Downy Woodpecker and
American Coot are good candidates for a third. A newly added
species needs a single stable identity so additional poses do not increase its
species selection weight or allow it to appear twice in one composition.
