# Special Days scene studies

Two AI-assisted illustrations for Emviary's Seasonal nature and A personal memory directions, generated November 23, 2026 from earlier AI-generated design studies in this project. No private family reference photographs were used.

Assets: `src/emviary/special_day_assets/season.png` and `memory.png`. Both have transparent margins so the tree, mountains and canyon end naturally within the composition instead of being clipped at a rectangular image edge. The actual renderer composites onto white paper before six-color conversion.

Season: complete evergreen, blue snow ridge and low yellow sun. Memory: imagined juniper, red canyon, winding river and path. The canyon is a study, not a claim about a real place. Personal-place art can be selected from the existing uploaded art collection.

Prompts requested broad flat shapes, restrained green/blue/red/yellow, complete silhouettes, transparent safety padding, no lettering, frame, paper grain or backgrounds. Rendered messages remain editable text. These scenes are never added automatically to an existing saved event.
